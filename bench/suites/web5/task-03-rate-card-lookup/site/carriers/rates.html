Zone Base Per billable kg

Billable weight

Weight is rounded up to the next whole kilogram. The minimum billable weight is 1 kg.

Surcharges

Zone C parcels carry a remote-area surcharge of 2.50 per parcel. Parcels over 30 kg are not accepted.

Price = base + per-kg rate × billable kg + surcharges, rounded to cents.
